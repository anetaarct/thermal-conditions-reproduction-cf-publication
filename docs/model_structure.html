Model formulas and diagnostics

Reproductive models

Both temperature formulations appear in the main analysis. The single-temperature model estimates one temperature association; the within–between model replaces it with separate within-female and between-female terms. The complete formulas and scripts are on the clutch, fledging and within–between pages.

Response Distribution and components Random intercepts
Clutch size Mean-parameterised COM-Poisson; predictors enter log mean and log inverse shape Female and year in mean
Egg-to-fledging success Zero-inflated beta-binomial; predictors enter component logit mean and log precision Female and year in mean; year in precision and extra-zero probability
Calendar-year trends Same response distributions as above, with temperature removed from location and scale Retained from corresponding reproductive model

Fitting requires curated individual records and saved starting objects. These are not included in the public repository, so model fitting is done locally in the recorded R/glmmTMB environment. The complete code is available as downloadable R scripts on the analysis pages.

Spring-temperature model

The environmental model is a Gaussian location–scale fit to the 46 annual mean temperatures. Calendar year enters both the mean and log standard deviation. This light model can be rerun in the browser from the public annual file:

Run R

Fit spring-temperature location and scale

Fits the Gaussian mean and log-SD likelihood to 46 annual temperatures. Coefficients for time are per decade; this is not a reproductive model.

The fitted warming trend was 0.0439°C per year (95% CI 0.0248–0.0630); residual interannual variability showed no clear temporal change.

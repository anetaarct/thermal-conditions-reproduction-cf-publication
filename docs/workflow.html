Refit limits


The fitted clutch-size and fledging models use curated individual breeding records and saved model objects. Those individual-level records and fitted objects are not in the public repository. The full fitting scripts and their required input names remain visible and downloadable on the model pages; they cannot produce the publication fits from the public aggregate files alone. The browser steps run checks and calculations on supplied public files; they do not pretend to refit models without their data.


The sliding-window analysis also begins from annual laying-date summaries; individual laying dates are not public. Its public cache retains aggregate candidate-window results and 500 saved randomised searches. The page shows which operations can be repeated from the public snapshot and which require the original field records.


Weather covers 1980–2025 (46 seasons), the sliding-window laying-date series covers 45 seasons, clutch-size data cover 12,627 breeding attempts from 8,894 females (1981–2025, excluding 2020), and fledging data cover 9,790 attempts from 7,132 females (1981–2019).






Software used for the reported fits


The reported reproductive fits used R 4.6.1 and glmmTMB 1.1.14. The within–between refits and exports used R 4.6.1 and glmmTMB 1.1.15.2. Browser calculations run in WebR and base R; they reproduce the indicated checks from public files, not the historical glmmTMB fits.
